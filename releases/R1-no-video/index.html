VairifyAnonymity With Accountability +

Vairify uses V.A.I. technology to protect user anonymity while maintaining a verified record of activity within its community.

Walkthrough · How Vairify Uses V.A.I.
01 Special Requirements Law-enforcement declaration Background screening
02 Binding Agreements Mutual-consent agreements
03 Platform Protocol Every binding request or agreement requires confirmation through a live facial scan.
04 User Anonymity Guaranteed by ChainPass. Vairify never receives or stores user identity. Its policy is to protect anonymity and not authorize identity disclosure. Its international structure and ChainPass’s zero-knowledge architecture add further separation between a user’s verified identity and the platform.
05 Platform Disclosure Policy Vairify does not authorize the release of user identity. Based in Colombia, its international structure further limits exposure to any single jurisdiction.
AV ChexxAge Compliance +

AV Chexx provides verified proof of age for the adult-film industry, reducing age-verification liability for studios.

Walkthrough · How AV Chexx Uses V.A.I.
01 Special Requirements Signed release authorizing AV Chexx to confirm the user’s age to regulatory authorities
02 Platform Protocol The performer submits a V.A.I. number. AV Chexx verifies the performer’s age and records the confirmation.
03 User Anonymity Guaranteed by ChainPass. AV Chexx never receives or stores your identity during normal use. However, participation requires a signed release authorizing identity disclosure when requested by the appropriate regulatory authorities.
04 Platform Disclosure Policy AV Chexx requires users of its platform to sign a release authorizing the disclosure of their identity to governing authorities when requested for age verification.
