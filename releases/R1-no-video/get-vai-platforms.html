Everything Your Platform Needs—Built In.

Every V.A.I. platform begins with verified users, secure onboarding, and zero-knowledge identity architecture. ChainPass manages the security infrastructure so your platform can focus on its users, experience, and growth.

01

Trusted Identity Verification

Every user is verified through a licensed KYC provider. A government ID is validated, and a live photo confirms that it belongs to the person submitting it. ChainPass retains only the verified photo; the identity documents remain with the provider.

02

Verified Signature Capability

Every V.A.I. includes a signature agreement, allowing users to confirm face-authenticated agreements, declarations, transactions, and authorizations with a facial scan—without revealing their identity to the platform.

03

Requirements Built Around Your Platform

Choose the requirements ChainPass agrees to administer for your platform. If a capability does not already exist, ChainPass will build it for your platform—at no cost.

04

Platform-Controlled Identity Release

Any identity release requires your platform’s authorization and submission of its session key through ChainPass—the only party authorized to retrieve identification from the KYC provider.

05

One Verified Anonymous Identity

Each user receives a unique seven-character V.A.I. number bound to their verified photo. Your platform can positively verify the user and shape its own experience without receiving or storing their identity.

06

Free Platform Integration

A complete API suite and client dashboard bring ChainPass verification, onboarding, and platform requirements into your product. There are no integration or platform fees. The V.A.I. holder pays.
