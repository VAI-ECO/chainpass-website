1. Who we are

ChainPass [legal entity name, OPEN] is a Delaware corporation. ChainPass verifies identity and age for platforms and issues the V.A.I. (Verified Anonymous Identity). This policy covers chainpass.io, the ChainPass enrolment pages, the retrieval and recovery pages, and the ChainPass API.

ChainPass is the controller of the identity data it verifies. When ChainPass collects a contact detail or username because a platform asked for it, ChainPass acts for that platform and hands the detail to the platform.

3. Your biometric data

ChainPass asks for your consent to collect and use your face data before any image is captured. The consent is ChainPass's own agreement with you and is separate from any platform's terms. You can decline. Without it a V.A.I. cannot be issued.

ChainPass uses your face template only to confirm that the person presenting a V.A.I. is the person who enrolled. ChainPass does not sell, lease or trade biometric data and does not profit from it in any other way. ChainPass does not share your face template with any platform.

ChainPass deletes face data on the earliest of: the purpose ending, [OPEN: period] after your last interaction with ChainPass, or the date required by law. [OPEN item 3: this must be reconciled with the append-only baseline rule before publishing.]

4. What a platform receives

When you finish enrolment from a platform, ChainPass hands that platform:

  • Your V.A.I. number.
  • A session key, delivered once. ChainPass deletes its own copy at the handoff.
  • The contact details and username the platform asked ChainPass to collect.
  • The results the platform is set to receive: a yes/no, a colour band, or a colour band with a match percentage.

A platform does not receive your face template or your ID document from ChainPass. [OPEN item 2: state here whether, when, and under what process the platform may use the session key to obtain your legal identity from the verification provider.]

Each platform's terms say what that platform requires ChainPass to collect and what it does with the result. You accept those terms on the ChainPass acceptance page before your V.A.I. is activated, and on your first visit to any other platform.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent. Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and other US states have rights under their state laws. Residents of the EU, UK and Switzerland have rights under the GDPR and equivalent laws.

Some records cannot be changed: a signed agreement record is kept as signed. Deleting your identity data ends your V.A.I.

Send requests to support@chainpass.io. ChainPass will confirm it is you with a face check or your recovery details before acting. ChainPass does not discriminate against you for using these rights. You may complain to your local data protection authority.

8. International transfers

ChainPass processes data in the United States. Where the law requires it, transfers from the EU, UK or Switzerland rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

9. Age

ChainPass issues a V.A.I. only to adults aged 18 or older, or the age of majority where you live if higher. ChainPass does not knowingly collect data from minors except the document check that refuses them.

10. Changes

ChainPass will post changes here with a new effective date. A material change is shown to you before it applies.
